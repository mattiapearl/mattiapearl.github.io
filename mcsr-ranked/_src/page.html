Limits & Ranked rules
  • No human end-to-end validation. Solver time excludes building, recording and travel.
  • No Ranked approval. Manual input is not an exemption.
  • M01 recovers full 64-bit RNG seeds. When that is the world seed, the full-world-seed prohibition applies. Hiding the output does not avoid it.
  • Version, standardization and per-method assumptions matter. None of these percentages measures player accuracy.
Unsuccessful shortcuts

Coarse drop bins: 180 s timeouts. Bush/glowstone/melon reversal: 360/120/120 s timeouts—not impossibility proofs. Fortune III always yields flint, revealing no flint/gravel pattern. Ordinary hoppers lose drop order. No remote F3/pie eye counter found.
