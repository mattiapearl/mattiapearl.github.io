02

RECORDED INPUTS → WORK RATIOS → POINTS

Three parts. One calculation per part.

Take changes in recorded counters between snapshots, express eligible work per 180 observed seconds, and normalize using fixed historical references. Call the resulting work ratio r. It is not a percentile or a comparison with the current lobby.

F / UP TO 40

Fighting & support

Records: hero damage, teammate healing and barriers, kills, assists and team health.

Rule: health-normalized damage/support mixed with kill participation. Barriers count half; the blend is 80 / 20.

O / UP TO 40

Objectives

Records: boss damage between snapshots, structure completions, reported claims and death windows.

Rule: a shrunk damage share at eligible friendly completions. It is interval evidence, not per-target attribution.

E / UP TO 20

Economy

Records: lane/neutral gold and creep, neutral and deny counters.

Rule: personal farm receipts backed by recorded farming work. Total wealth does not earn extra points.

component points= maximum × r1 + r

r = 0 → zero points
r = 1 → half the maximum
r = 3 → three quarters

Why this curve? It keeps each part within its budget without a hard cutoff. More work still earns more points, but progressively less. That compresses legitimate high production too; the benchmark did not establish this particular shape as best.

Exact source fields, work definitions and reference values

Counters come from players[].stats[]. Δ denotes the change between adjacent snapshots. T is last observed clock − first observed clock, using time_stamp_s; it is not automatically the full match duration. HP below is the previous snapshot’s team median maximum health.

ONE ADDITIONAL CHOICE

Account for enemy gains during absence.

The current formula discounts gross work when a recorded enemy structure completion or reported Rejuvenator claim coincides with the player being dead and their team having more players dead. A death alone does not trigger a deduction.

YOUR SHARE OF ONE EVENT

(3 − 1) / (6 × 3) = 0.1111

One ninth for each of three dead teammates; one third allocated across the team. The remainder stays unallocated.

L = Σ shares × 180 / T
P = 1 / (1 + 0.4 × L / CO)

Concession units 0.1111Factor P 0.9529

Living players receive zero share. No enemy gain, equal dead counts or fewer own dead also means zero. Each structure/claim counts once as one unit, not measured utility.

At this factor, 20 gross points lose 0.94; 80 gross points lose 3.77. The proportional rule makes the second deduction four times larger for the same event pattern. That is not a claim that high producers lose more across the actual cohort.

Death state uses death_details[].game_time_s and death_duration_s. The Rejuvenator beneficiary is the reported claimant, not necessarily the killer. The boss-kill clock is a claim-timing proxy, not a pickup timestamp. The claim is counted once.

    What this candidate does not promise

    One exact number for every player; precise per-target or pickup attribution; positioning, trades, clutches, useful sacrifices or causal blame. Names, rank and winning do not add bonuses.

    Current input support is Ranked, twelve players, six per team. The benchmark does not establish custom-match compatibility or accuracy in another population.

    Technical requirements and reproducibility

    Input needs explicit death/objective lists and at least two common-clock snapshots covering both 60 seconds and half the match. Missing lists are not assumed empty. No held metadata means an unavailable result; no automatic acquisition is illustrated here.

    The four synthetic rosters come from the byte-pinned scoring artifact. The sliders illustrate its arithmetic; the benchmark JSON contains aggregate results only. No real player identities, raw match data, credentials, analytics, third-party runtime code or live scoring calls are published here.
